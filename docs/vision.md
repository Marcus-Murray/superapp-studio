# Vision

You want a sleek, modern superapp that merges the creative flow of Figma with the organizational power of Notion. Core outcomes:

- Tablet-first ideation: sketch, flowchart, whiteboard, and wireframe on Samsung S10 FE+.
- Notion-like workspace: pages, blocks, databases, tasks, kanban, calendar.
- AI code generation: convert ideas/designs into repo boilerplates that sync with your IDE.
- Seamless sync: work out in the world, then sit at PC and continue where you left off.
- World-class engineering: clean, modular architecture with strong typing, testing, and DX.
